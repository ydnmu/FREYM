# FREYM trademark policy

The official project repository is [ydnmu/FREYM](https://github.com/ydnmu/FREYM).
This policy covers the FREYM project name and its project logos, including the
legacy Frame name when used to identify this project. It makes no claim to every
ordinary use of the word "frame" and does not claim trademark registration.

The software is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later; see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and
[NOTICE](NOTICE). You may use, study, modify, fork and redistribute it under that
license, including commercially. Copyright permission does not by itself grant
trademark permission. This policy addresses branding and confusion about origin;
it adds no restriction on the software rights granted by the GPL.

Keep required copyright and license notices even when renaming a fork. A truthful
credit such as "Based on FREYM" with an upstream link is welcome and does not
require permission. Such a promotional credit is not an additional condition of
the software license.

## Permitted references

You may accurately name the project in documentation, comparisons, reviews,
compatibility descriptions and attribution. You may identify an unmodified
redistribution by its original name when its source and distributor are clear
and you do not imply that the project produced, reviewed or supports your package.
Uses permitted by applicable law are unaffected.

## Modified products and endorsement

Use a distinct product name and icon for a modified public product so users can
tell it apart from official builds. An explanatory reference to its FREYM origin
is permitted. Do not use the project name or logos as your own product identity,
domain identity or promotional endorsement in a way likely to confuse users.

Do not describe a fork or third-party package as an official FREYM release, or
claim project approval, sponsorship or support without permission. Do not remove
required attribution to conceal upstream authorship.

For proposed branding outside these permissions, contact the maintainers through
the official repository. No donation, commercial license purchase or copyright
assignment is required to exercise GPL rights.

## Scope

Only actual trademark rights are reserved; third-party marks remain with their
owners. This policy is separate from, and does not amend, the GPL. The distinction
follows [GPLv3 section 7(e)](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.en.html#section7).
